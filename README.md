<h1>Bubble Berry Acres</h1>

<h2>Description</h2>
Hunger is a global issue, affecting nearly 1 in 10 people. It impacts people’s ability to stay healthy mentally and physically. Often to be associated with the lack of food, hunger can also be a byproduct of food waste and lack of access to healthy food. To solve the hunger problem, we need to improve how we produce and distribute food. That’s where you come in! A fundamental part of solving world hunger is addressing it in our local community. You are the proud new owner of Bubble-Berry Farms! Using your only leftover gold, you must buy, grow, and sell healthy fresh foods to the bubble citizens of your local community.  Don’t stop there though! Mysterious machines and secrets only known to the future may help you save the world.
